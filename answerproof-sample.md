<section class="answerproof-band">
    <div class="section-label"><span class="prompt">$</span> interpretation</div>
    <div class="answerproof-offer-grid">
        <div class="answerproof-panel">
            <h2>What showed up</h2>
            <ul class="answerproof-list">
                <li>AI answers blended product pages, launch pages, directories, and comparison content.</li>
                <li>Third-party sources mattered, not just vendor-owned pages.</li>
                <li>The answer favored pages that already explained the category in buyer language.</li>
            </ul>
        </div>
        <div class="answerproof-panel">
            <h2>What I would check next</h2>
            <ul class="answerproof-list">
                <li>Whether the same vendors appear across Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI surfaces.</li>
                <li>Which source types repeat across high-intent buyer questions.</li>
                <li>Where a target company is absent despite having strong SEO pages.</li>
            </ul>
        </div>
    </div>
</section>
