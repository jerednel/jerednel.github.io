I ran 12 small AI-search pre-checks while building Answerproof.

These were intentionally narrow: one buyer question, one target domain, a few
competitor domains, and a live AI answer/citation check. Not a full diagnostic.
Just enough signal to decide whether a deeper audit is worth discussing.

The pattern was useful immediately.

Some companies were absent while adjacent competitors or sources appeared:

- A capital advisory query did not mention 30A Capital, while adjacent capital
  advisory sources appeared.
- An analytics consulting query did not mention DF Insights, while Mammoth
  Growth appeared.
- A CMMC compliance query did not mention Hatty AI, while TrustNovaOps appeared
  and was cited.

Some companies did appear, which changes the sales question. The question is no
longer "are we invisible?" It becomes:

```text
Do we keep showing up across long-tail buyer questions?
Which incumbents appear beside us?
Which source pages cause the answer?
What would make the answer more defensible next month?
```

That is why I am positioning Answerproof as an audit-first diagnostic, not
another monitoring dashboard.

The first deliverable should not be a graph. It should be a source-backed answer
to this question:

> When buyers ask AI systems who they should compare, do they see you,
> competitors, or nobody useful?

The fixed-scope diagnostic is $1,500:

- 10 buyer questions
- up to 3 competitor domains
- AI answer and citation review
- source trail
- 30-day implementation plan

Best fit: B2B SaaS, data consultancies, AI compliance/GRC, financial services,
and high-value B2B services where one qualified lead matters.
